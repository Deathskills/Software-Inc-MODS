Agreed rules

  1. Zero stays zero. When a 5-year point is 0, the rate stays 0 until the next point, then jumps to it (Console OS is 0 until 2000, then 0.1).
  2. The rate follows the current year. A product released in 2003 and still used in 2015 uses the 2015 rate, to reflect rising costs and better technology.
  3. After 2025, baselines keep growing in a straight line at their 2020–2025 rate per year (for example, Computer OS +27 per year, Office cloud +76 per year).
  4. Multiplayer 1990–2000 goes in a straight line from vanilla's 62 to your 120.
  5. MMO is the whole game (Multiplayer + MMO). It goes in a straight line from vanilla's 477 in 1990 to 900 in 2020, then grows 5% every 5 years like Multiplayer.
  6. Office. Ticking Network adds the "Office offline" rate. Adding Cloud backup also adds the "Office (cloud)" rate, on top of vanilla Cloud backup.
  7. OS baseline applies to every OS, whether or not Network is ticked. (Network is forced on Computer and Phone OS and optional on Console OS.)
  8. Business stars still matter, as in vanilla: profit per Mbps ranges from 80% at 0 stars to 120% at 5 stars around your targets. These tables show the 100% midpoint.
