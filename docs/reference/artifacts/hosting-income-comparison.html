2. Hosting offers per year and their potential profit

This is an estimate. The game doesn't have a fixed number of AI releases. Company counts come from the game's company types (Games 3–6 companies, Computer OS 2, Console OS 2–3, Phone OS 2–3, Antivirus 2, Office 2). How often they release and which features they pick are my assumptions, and you can edit them below.

Mod offers / yearVanilla offers / year

How offers are counted. Vanilla: one offer per AI release that has a vanilla server feature, at any size. Mod: those same offers, plus one for every unhosted AI product (subsidiaries of AI companies included) that needs more than 1 Mbps. Each one can ask again every 6 months.

Potential profit is the monthly profit you'd add if you accepted every offer from that year, with every product at the chosen userbase. Deals made in earlier years keep paying on top of this.
