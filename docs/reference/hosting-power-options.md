# Hosting power options

> Exported from the project artifact "Hosting Power Options" (13 Nov 2026). Original page: [artifacts/hosting-power-options.html](artifacts/hosting-power-options.html). Outcome: Marc picked the fixed **$7 per Mbps per month** power price (PR #3).

How much you keep from hosting deals if the mod charges a fixed power cost of $4, $6 or $7 per Mbps per month, compared with vanilla and with the mod as it is today.

Assumptions:

- January 1992
- Your city map: ISP $17.10 / Mbps / month
- 3 business stars unless stated
- Tower servers: real power $1.73 / Mbps / month
- Game dollars (USD)

"Today" is the version you're playing, which charges $1.73 for power. Vanilla's profit is its roughly 17% markup at 3 stars, about $3.14 per Mbps a month. Vanilla wouldn't host an OS at all without add-ons, so its column shows what it would pay for the same load. Profit means income minus ISP and the real power your Towers use. Payback shows full load / 50% / 10% where three values are given.

## Wind turbine

The power option doesn't change this. The numbers come from the turbine's screenshot and your current electricity bill.

| Item | Value |
|---|---|
| Price | $1,000,000 (you quoted $1.25M) |
| Best-case savings, using all of it yourself | $19,440 / month |
| Your average use today | about 2.4 kW (about $1,000 / month) |
| Surplus sold to the grid | 25% of the price |
| Realistic value today | about $5,600 / month |
| Payback today | 15 to 18 years, past its 48-month life |
| Payback at full own use | 51 months ($1M) to 64 months ($1.25M) |

It starts to make sense only at a steady 45 kW or more of your own load, which is about 11 fully loaded Towers (11 Gbps).
